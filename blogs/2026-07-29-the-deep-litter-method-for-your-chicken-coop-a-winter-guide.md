Learning how to use the deep litter method in the chicken coop can benefit your flock and make cold-weather chicken keeping a whole lot easier on you. Instead of hauling bedding out of the coop every other week, you let the litter do the work. It manages moisture, keeps ammonia under control, insulates the coop floor, and gives you compost for the garden, all with very little maintenance.
